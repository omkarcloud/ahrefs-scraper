YouTube
YouTube is a video sharing platform that hosts user-uploaded and professionally produced content across a wide range of genres. It enables uploading, viewing, liking, commenting on, and sharing videos, subscribing to channels, and organizing content into playlists and recommendations. The service supports live streaming, shorts, and long-form videos, with search and discovery features, personalized recommendations, regional catalogs, and a variety of content categories including music, education, entertainment, news, and tutorials. Creator tools provide channel management, analytics, monetization options, and audience engagement, while accessibility features such as captions and transcripts enhance reach.
